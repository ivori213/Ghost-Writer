Before you start — read this.

Ghost Writer uses Claude AI to draft your H1 2026 self-review. Read carefully before continuing.

Ghost Writer searches your connected tools — Granola, Slack, Notion, and others — and drafts a self-review based on what it finds. It has no idea what your manager thinks of you, what came up in undocumented conversations, or how your work landed relative to peers.

Ghost Writer is designed to only credit work you personally drove. It checks who said what and who owned what in every meeting note — your manager's decisions and your peers' deliverables do not raise your rating. But it's not perfect. Review every claim before you submit.

To get the most accurate review: connect your tools before starting, and use the optional context fields to fill in anything that isn't captured in writing.

⚠ Ghost Writer tends to rate generously. If a rating feels too high, it probably is — adjust before submitting.
